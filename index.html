Level design project · 3D first-person narrative

Heritage

An orphan explores the house he inherited from a family he never met. Two enclosed levels, the ground floor and a dark labyrinthine cellar, where progress is gated by keys and light.

  • Critical and optional paths: the keycard opens the hidden cellar; the flashlight and lighter (for wall torches) make the cellar readable.
  • Two-act tension: an ordinary but uneasy house, then a cellar that reveals its secrets and ends at a mysterious portal.
  • Full documentation: 2D layout and legend, flow chart, beat chart, room-by-room objectives and an asset list of 40 items.
  • Built and tested as a 3D whitebox.

Game concept & MVP plan · slides

Rescue Rush

A hybrid-casual puzzle concept and MVP plan, prepared for Noktune. The question: can a familiar unblock + match-3 layout feel tense without a timer or an enemy?

  • Hypothesis: player-driven, conditional tension. Each choice fills a limited buffer and narrows the safe options.
  • Rescue framing as the marketable hook, layered on without touching the core loop.
  • A 20-level curve in four stages: learn, expand, manage, master.
  • Three-week sprint plan, a must-have vs nice-to-have backlog and the analytics signals that would validate it.
